What I Learned
01

Most platforms treat memberships as a feature. The best ones treat it as the entire product. That difference shows up in every UX decision.

02

A Med Spa owner doesn't need enterprise complexity. They need speed to revenue and clarity on their numbers every morning.

03

The clinics I've seen grow fastest are the ones with recurring revenue before they scale — not after. Membership is infrastructure, not a loyalty bonus.

04

If patients can't easily see, manage, or value their membership online, churn doubles. The patient portal experience matters as much as billing.
